1810 – 1851 · THE EARLY YEARS

Early History

Born in Prince Edward Island in 1810, George Coles rose from humble beginnings as a brewer and merchant to become one of the island's most influential voices for responsible government.

Entering the House of Assembly in 1842, he fought tirelessly against absentee landlordism and championed voting rights for ordinary Islanders.

His Legacy

As the first Premier of Prince Edward Island and a Father of Confederation, Coles helped shape free public education, land reform, and modern democratic governance.
